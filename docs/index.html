✂️ 4 Multi-Strategy Chunking Paradigms
Eliminating naive fixed-length splitting to maximize semantic retrieval quality:

🪟 1. Fixed-Size with 20% Sliding Overlap

256-token boundaries with 51-token sliding windows to guarantee entity continuity and prevent context fragmentation across boundaries.

🔤 2. Sentence-Boundary Aware Splitting

Linguistic tokenization ensuring passage chunks never sever compound clauses or technical terminology midway through sentences.

📄 3. Paragraph & Structure-Aware Splitting

Preserves structural paragraph markers to maintain topical cohesion for multi-aspect questions and definitions.

🧠 4. Semantic Similarity Clustering

Measures adjacent embedding cosine variance dynamically to aggregate semantically dense sentences into unified vectors.
